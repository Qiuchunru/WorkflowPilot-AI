
Project Name:

E-Commerce Platform Launch



Project Description:

A company plans to build and launch a new
e-commerce platform to improve online sales
and customer experience.



Project Goal:

Create a scalable online shopping platform
with product management, customer accounts,
payment processing, and analytics features.



Team:

- 3 Software Developers
- 1 UI/UX Designer
- 1 Product Manager
- 1 Marketing Specialist



Deadline:

3 months



Constraints:

- Limited development resources
- Need continuous customer feedback
- Integration with existing business systems
- Fast delivery requirement



Current Challenges:

- Managing multiple work streams
- Prioritizing important features
- Coordinating communication between teams
- Reducing repetitive project management tasks
